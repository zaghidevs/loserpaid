questions

is this a joke?

the concept is. the payouts aren’t. every round, real sol goes to the wallet that lost the most.

where does the pot come from?

pump.fun pays the coin creator a small fee on every trade. those fees go into the pot. the number on this page is an estimate until the fees are claimed.

can i fake a loss to win?

you’d lose more than you win. a winner gets back at most 50% of what they lost. tokens have to be held 5+ minutes before a sell counts, and tokens sent in from another wallet don’t count at all.

do i need to claim anything?

no. the payout is sent straight to the wallet that lost the most.

how do i know people actually get paid?

every paid round in the hall of losers links to its transaction on solscan. check it yourself.

what if nobody loses?

then nobody wins, and the whole pot rolls into the next round.
